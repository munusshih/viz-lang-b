<div class="due-date">Due: April 21, 2026</div>

We will spend the next four weeks publishing everything we have done as a collective doc, and share it with the public. This is an opportunity to reflect on our work, share our process and impact with others, and create a lasting record of our collective action and engagement.

<br>

![alt text](/assets/projects/publication.jpg)
Image from [Fortunately Magazine](https://fortunately.us/)

<br>

The collective doc can take many forms, such as a zine, a newspaper, several posters, a website, or any other format that you think would be effective in sharing your work with others. The important thing is to create something that captures the essence of your collective and the impact of your engagement, and that can be shared with a wider audience. Consider how you can use design to make your collective doc engaging, accessible, and impactful. Think about how you can use visuals, storytelling, and other design elements to communicate your message and inspire others to take action.

The collective doc should include, but not be limited to, the following elements:

- The Name of the Collective
- Why you formed the collective and what are your values and intentions as a collective?
- The people in the collective and their roles. You can include a brief bio of each member, their skills and interests, and how they contribute to the collective.
- Your original manifesto
- The process of drafting and revising your manifesto
- Your ways of working together as a collective
- How has the ways of working together evolved over time? Any challenges you have faced and how you have overcome them?
- Conflict resolution: How have you resolved conflicts within your collective? What strategies have you used to navigate disagreements and maintain a positive working relationship?
- Your collective activation project, including the process of designing and executing your engagement, the impact of your engagement, and any reflections you have on the experience.
- Any other reflections, insights, or learnings you have gained from this experience.
- Documentation of your process, such as photos, videos, written reflections.
- Any other materials that you think would be relevant and interesting to share with others.
