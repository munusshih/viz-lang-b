## Class Activity

This week we're meeting at [Secret Riso Club](https://secretrisoclub.com/) for a studio visit. Many of you already work with Risograph, so this is a chance to see how a working artist-run studio operates day-to-day, and to share your own publications with the team.

<div class="due-date">May 5, 2026 · 5:00–7:30 PM</div>

**What to bring**

- A printed (or digital) copy of your collective publication
- A few questions for Tara about running the studio, publishing as a collective, or becoming a member

**Agenda**

- **5:00–5:30 PM** — Arrive and tour the space
- **5:30–6:15 PM** — Talk from Tara on the history of Secret Riso Club and how the studio developed its publishing work, followed by Q&A
- **6:00–7:00 PM** — You'll present your publications and share what your collective has been working on
- **7:00–7:30 PM** — Open conversation and wrap-up
